# LumaChart — Plans & Long-Term Value

**Doctors and health systems buy value over years, not a feature checklist.** In-app: the **Plans & value** view (clinician → Platform).

## Pricing model (illustrative — no fabricated figures)

| Plan | Price | For |
|---|---|---|
| **Solo** | $0 to start | Solo & very small practices — cloud EHR + portal, evidence-based prevention, agent-assisted coding |
| **Practice** | 30-day free trial, then flexible per-provider pricing | Small–mid practices — adds revenue analytics, digital payments, interoperability (labs/e-Rx/eligibility), priority support |
| **Enterprise** | Custom, per site | Health systems — adds inpatient + enterprise integration, TEFCA/QHIN exchange, the CWO well-being suite, dedicated success + BAA |

Free trials and flexible pricing lower the barrier for small practices; enterprise terms are negotiated.

## The four long-term-value pillars

1. **Free trial & flexible pricing** — $0 to start, a 30-day trial, transparent per-provider pricing that scales — not a big up-front license.
2. **Cloud-based access** — secure from any location/device, no servers to buy or maintain → lower total cost of ownership over the life of the practice. (Trade-off: shared security responsibility — see [TRUST-SECURITY-DEPLOYMENT.md](TRUST-SECURITY-DEPLOYMENT.md).)
3. **Compliance & security** — HIPAA-aligned by design (encryption, audit trails, RBAC, MFA, signed BAA); SOC 2 + ONC certification on the roadmap.
4. **Future-ready technology** — evidence-linked decision support, workflow automation, and revenue analytics that get **more valuable over time** as guidelines update and the consented research network grows.

## Why the value compounds

A feature list is copied in a quarter. An **evidence-based record** improves every time a guideline updates, every time the consented research network grows, and every time it prevents a denial or an after-hours hour. LumaChart's differentiator — *evidence on the EHR, not just AI on the EHR* ([COMPETITIVE-LANDSCAPE.md](COMPETITIVE-LANDSCAPE.md)) — is the value that accrues, not the feature that's matched.
